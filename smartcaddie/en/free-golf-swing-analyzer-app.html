To improve at golf, hitting more balls is not enough. You need to understand what happens in the swing: club path, plane, rhythm, body position and repeatability. SmartCaddie answers a very direct search intent: a free golf swing analyzer app, with no external sensor and no advertising.

Why video analysis changes practice

Feel is often misleading in golf. A player may feel the club returning from the inside while video shows an outside-in path. SmartCaddie helps turn that feeling into visible evidence through phone-camera analysis and AI visual cues.

No sensor, no heavy setup

SmartCaddie relies on computer vision. Your phone becomes the core tool: frame the swing, run the analysis and review the important checkpoints. To start well, follow the guide on how to film your golf swing for AI.

An app that helps you work with your coach

The goal is not to replace an instructor. Good video analysis can make lessons more productive: you arrive with examples, history and sharper questions. Your coach keeps the expertise, while SmartCaddie gives you visual memory between sessions.

Where to begin

  • Record one face-on swing and one down-the-line swing.
  • Compare your checkpoints with the P1 to P10 swing positions.
  • Choose one focus area: slice, tempo, grip or plane.
  • Repeat across sessions to track change over time.
