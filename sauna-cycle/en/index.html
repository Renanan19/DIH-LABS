Guided Cycles

Heat, cold and rest phases with clear timers. Follow your protocol without watching the clock.

Thermal Adaptation Score

A 0–100 score built from your consistency, cold tolerance and session discipline — watch it grow over weeks.

Weekly charts of your sessions, exposure time and score, plus streaks to keep the habit alive.

Total Privacy

No ads, no account, no tracking. Your sessions and health data stay on your device. Always.
