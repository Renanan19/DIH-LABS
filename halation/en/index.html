One album, three promises

Free for everyone. Nothing on our machines. A lock that does not depend on trusting us.

The link is the album

Make an album, send one link to the group chat, and everyone shoots into it. No account, no password, no group to configure.

No server holds your photos

Phones talk to each other directly, encrypted. There is no photo store to breach, and no bill that grows with popularity — which is how it stays free.

Sealed until a date

Lock an album as a time capsule. The key does not exist until the date, so nobody can look early — including the person who made it, and including us.

24 cinema film looks

Aurora, Nightfall, Noir, Tungsten, Gold and 19 more. Full colour transforms baked as 3D LUTs, not a tint laid over the top. Photos and video.

Grain, halation, leaks, frames

Four grain stocks sized to the format, nine frames from 35 mm to Pano, dust, scratches and light leaks. Every layer has its own strength.

Photos, videos, Live Photos

Albums carry photos, videos and Live Photos, and a Live Photo stays a Live Photo: hold it and it moves. iPhone and Android share the same engine, so the invite, the encryption and the time lock are identical on both.

What it does, and what it costs you

  • ✓

    Anyone with the link is in

    There is no guest list to check, so treat the link like a key and send it only to people who should be in the album.

  • ✓

    Photos travel phone to phone

    A photo reaches the others when its phone and theirs are online around the same time. After an event, open the app on Wi-Fi once or twice.

  • ✓

    A public relay, sometimes

    In roughly 10–30% of cases two phones cannot connect directly and a public relay forwards encrypted traffic. It cannot read the photos. It is the one third-party machine involved.

  • ✓

    Editing works offline

    The looks live inside the app. Shared albums need a connection between phones; editing does not need one at all.

  • ✓

    It never reads your whole library

    iPhone is asked only for permission to save. On Android the system picker hands over just the photo you chose.

  • ✓

    No account. No ads. No watermark.

    Free. The App Store lists the app as Data Not Collected. Requires iOS 17 or later, or Android 10 or later.
