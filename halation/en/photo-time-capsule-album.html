The photos wait. Everyone opens together.

DIH LABS · Updated 11 October 2026

A shared album normally works the other way round: every photo is visible the moment it is taken. A time capsule keeps the photos out of sight so that opening them becomes the event.

Short answer: a time-capsule album is encrypted so that nobody can open it before the date — not the guests, not the person who made it, not the app's makers. On the date it opens for everyone at once. Halation lets you choose between that and a normal open album.

Why seal an album at all

Seeing photos as they arrive has real downsides on a big occasion. Early photos set the story, the best ones get seen out of order, and the people who could not be there watch the day happen in fragments. A sealed album turns the photographs into a single moment: everyone shoots, nobody peeks, and on the date everyone sees the whole day together.

It also changes how people photograph. Knowing nobody will see a frame for a week, guests take more pictures and fewer curated ones.

A promise is not a lock

Most "reveal later" features are a promise. The photos sit on a server, a setting hides them, and an administrator — or anyone who finds a way around the setting — can look. That is a rule, and a rule can be broken or changed.

A time capsule built on time-lock encryption is different: the photographs are encrypted to a future moment, and the key needed to open them does not exist yet. It will be produced by a public randomness beacon on the chosen date. Until then there is nothing to leak, bribe or subpoena.

What you choose

SettingEffect
OpenPhotos are visible as they arrive. Nothing is sealed.
Sealed until a date and timePhotos can be added by members, but nobody can open them before the moment you set.

Sealing is optional by design. The date exists because the reveal is worth waiting for, not because secrecy is the point.

Occasions that suit a capsule

  • Weddings: open on the morning after, with everyone at the breakfast table.
  • Stag and hen weekends: the evidence is released after everyone is home.
  • Graduations and farewells: a class or a team opens it together a year later.
  • Trips: a long journey with friends, opened on the way back.

Two things to know. Set the date and time carefully: a capsule cannot be brought forward, and that is the point. And once the album has opened, it is an ordinary album — photos that members added before the date are readable by everyone who holds the link.
