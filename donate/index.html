Why the Lightning Network?

The Lightning Network is a payment network built on top of Bitcoin. Where a regular on-chain Bitcoin transaction can take several minutes and cost a few euros in fees, a Lightning payment is:

  • Instant — the donation arrives within seconds;
  • Nearly free — fees amount to fractions of a cent;
  • Accessible — you can send from just a few satoshis (1 satoshi = 0.00000001 BTC);
  • Direct — no banking intermediary, no account to create, no personal data requested.

It is the ideal way to donate any amount you like, even a symbolic one — the equivalent of a coffee already covers part of our infrastructure costs.

How to donate in 3 steps

What is a Lightning address?

A Lightning address is a human-readable identifier in email format — like dihlabs@blink.sv — that lets you receive Lightning payments without generating an invoice each time. Your wallet automatically converts it into a payment request (LNURL-pay). It is today's simplest standard for Bitcoin donations and tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum amount?

No. You can send from just a few satoshis. Every donation counts.

Is the donation anonymous?

No personal data is requested: no account, no form. You send directly from your own wallet.

Can I get a tax receipt?

Not at the moment. Bitcoin donations support the association but do not currently qualify for a tax receipt.
