Spot burnout a week early.

Most burnout warnings arrive too late — after the bad week, the blowup, the crash. Pulse reads the quieter signals your body and your behaviour send days earlier, and turns them into one number you can act on.

One number, and the reasons behind it

A score alone is a horoscope. Pulse always shows what is driving it, how confident it is, and what to do next.

A daily risk score

0 to 100, from Steady to Critical, recomputed every day in the background from the data you already generate.

A 7-day forecast

A projection of where your risk is heading, drawn with a confidence band that widens honestly as the horizon grows.

Ranked drivers

The signals pushing your score up today, sorted by how much each one actually contributes. No mystery weighting.

One action at a time

The single highest-leverage change for today, plus the week's biggest movers. Each recommendation cites the source behind it.

A full history

Every day on one timeline, each one openable down to the individual metric. Trends you can scroll, not just a number of the day.

Private by design

No account, no cloud sync, no analytics. The database lives on your iPhone and nothing leaves it. Ever.

Fourteen signals, measured against you

  • ✓

    Your baseline, not a population average

    Pulse learns your own 30-day median for every signal, then measures each day against your history. Six hours of sleep means something different for you than for a stranger.

  • ✓

    Sleep, heart, habits, mindset

    Sleep duration, efficiency and timing. HRV and resting heart rate. Activity, phone use, late-night screens, work hours. Mindful minutes, plus mood, stress and energy from a 30-second check-in.

  • ✓

    Thirty seconds a day

    Connect Apple Health once, answer three quick questions daily. Pulse refreshes in the background and keeps a home-screen widget current.

  • ✓

    The method is published

    Robust z-scores, weighted sum, smoothing, linear forecast — the whole computation is written out in the app, and every health claim links to its source. It is an early-warning tool, not a diagnosis.
