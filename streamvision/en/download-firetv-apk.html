Install on Fire TV in 3 Minutes

The fastest route is the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews, orange icon) from the Amazon Appstore. Condensed steps:

1. Unlock Developer Options

On newer Fire OS versions they're hidden: go to Settings → My Fire TV → About and click your device name 7 times. Then open Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options.

2. Allow Downloader to install apps

Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore, then in Developer Options → Install unknown apps, enable it for Downloader (older Fire OS: switch "Apps from Unknown Sources" ON).

3. Type the short URL

Open Downloader and enter dih-labs.com/apk. The download page opens on your TV — click the big "Download APK" button, wait a few seconds (it's only 7.4 MB), then choose Install and Open. Downloader will offer to delete the APK file afterwards to free up space.

Why Sideload? Why Isn't It in the Amazon Appstore Yet?

Honest answer: the Fire TV build of StreamVision is currently distributed directly from our website while the Amazon Appstore listing is being prepared. Direct distribution lets us ship Fire TV fixes immediately — v31.0.0 adds runtime security hardening (anti-tampering protections and stripped debug metadata), building on v30's Fire TV compatibility work: MediaSession support so the remote's play/pause/rewind buttons work properly, and Google Cast disabled on Fire OS (which has no Play Services).

Sideloading an APK you obtained from its developer is legal and completely standard on Fire TV — Amazon itself ships the Developer Options and "Install unknown apps" toggles that make it possible. It's the same mechanism developers and testers use every day. The only real caveats: sideloaded apps don't auto-update (check back here for new versions), and you should only sideload APKs from sources you trust — which is exactly why we publish the SHA-256 checksum above.

On phones, tablets and Google TV you don't need to sideload at all: StreamVision is on Google Play, and an iPhone/iPad version is on the App Store.
