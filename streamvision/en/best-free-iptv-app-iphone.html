M3U or Xtream Codes on iPhone?

If your provider gives you a single long URL containing get.php, username= and password=, that is usually an M3U playlist URL generated by an Xtream-style panel. You can paste it as M3U, or extract the server, username and password and use Xtream Codes mode.

M3U is the easiest option when you have a small or personal playlist. Xtream Codes is usually better for large catalogs because categories, VOD, series and EPG data are requested in a more structured way. Legal and safety checklist

An IPTV player is just software. What matters is the content source you import. Avoid public "free channel" lists promising premium sports, movies or pay TV. Those lists are often unstable, unauthorized, privacy-invasive or simply broken. A safe IPTV setup should be boring: clear provider, clear rights, no suspicious profiles, no DNS changes, no forced VPN, and no app asking you to install unknown certificates.
